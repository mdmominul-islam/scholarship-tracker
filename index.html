How to use this: filter by “Full funding only” when you need a full ride, or by country when an application portal costs money. The timeline tab sorts every deadline chronologically so you can work backwards — scholarship deadlines are usually 1–4 months before the programme's own deadline.

Tuition, living costs and deadlines are indicative for the 2027 intake and move every year — always confirm on the university's own admissions page before you pay an application fee. Living-cost figures are a planning estimate, not a quote. “Full” means tuition plus a stipend is available through at least one of the listed schemes.

How updates work: countdowns and “passed” flags use today's date, so they refresh every time you open the page. New programmes and scholarships come from the data source: either the built-in list, or a live CSV link you set in CONFIG.DATA_URL (for example a Google Sheet published as CSV with the same columns as “Download CSV”).
